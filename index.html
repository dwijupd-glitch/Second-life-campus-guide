Handling batteries safely

Do

  • Keep spare batteries cool and dry
  • Tape over the contacts of loose batteries
  • Hand them to an authorized recycler
  • Use the campus battery bin

Do not

  • Throw them in a regular bin
  • Open, crush or puncture them
  • Keep charging a swollen device
  • Give them to unlicensed scrap buyers

Common questions

What counts as e-waste?

Anything with a plug, battery or circuit board that is no longer wanted: phones, laptops, chargers, cables, earphones, power banks and small appliances.

Why not just put it in the bin?

Devices contain metals and chemicals that can leak into soil and water, and batteries can start fires in waste trucks and dumps. Recyclers also recover valuable materials.

Is my data safe if I recycle it?

Not automatically. Back up, sign out and factory reset first. Use the checklist above.

How do I know a recycler is genuine?

Ask for their authorization from the state pollution control board and check it against the official list before you hand anything over.
